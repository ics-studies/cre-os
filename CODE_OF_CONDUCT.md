# Code of Conduct

ICS repositories are professional research and engineering spaces. Participants
must communicate respectfully, critique evidence rather than persons, protect
confidential information and avoid harassment, discrimination or disclosure of
private data.

Disagreement is welcome. Misrepresentation, coordinated abuse, doxxing,
credential sharing and intentional disruption are not.

Report conduct concerns privately to **members@ics-studies.org**. Reports will
be reviewed with attention to context, safety and the rights of affected
people.
